This document defines the contact routes, support scope, and basic response policy for GeneSIS and G.E.N.E.

## 1. Contact Routes

| Matter | Contact Route |
|---|---|
| Short questions about article content, reading order, or basic usage | Quick Questions page on note |
| Publicly shareable and reproducible general defects | GitHub Issues — Bug Report |
| Documentation errors or omissions | GitHub Issues — Documentation Feedback |
| Development suggestions or feedback | GitHub Issues — Development Feedback |
| Purchases, Founder verification, terms, rights, or privacy | Official GeneSIS contact |
| Non-public defects and other private matters | Official GeneSIS contact |
| Unresolved security issues | Private route described in the [Security Policy](../SECURITY.md) |

**Official GeneSIS Contact**

`genesis.official.poc@mail.com`

**Quick Questions (note)**

[Quick Questions article](https://note.com/genesis_protocol/n/n6a2a06ad2698)

The Quick Questions page accepts short questions about article content, reading order, or basic usage through public comments. Do not use it for formal defect reports, private inquiries, or security reports.

Comments are public. Do not post personal information, purchase information, credentials, private conversation history, details of unresolved vulnerabilities, or other non-public information.

The Quick Questions page does not guarantee a reply to every question or resolution of any issue.

Replies, mentions, and direct messages on X or other social media are not formal support channels.

## 2. GitHub Issues

GitHub Issues are public. When reporting a general defect, include the following information where reasonably possible:

- G.E.N.E version or build
- Operating environment, including OS, runtime, and model
- Summary of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Expected and actual behavior
- Frequency of occurrence
- Logs or screen information that can be shared publicly
- Any confirmed workaround

GeneSIS may be unable to investigate or reproduce a report when necessary information is unavailable.

Do not post the following in a public Issue:

- Personal information, purchase information, or Founder verification information
- Passwords, tokens, API keys, private keys, or authentication codes
- Private conversation history, Persona information, or non-public materials
- Details that could be used to reproduce or exploit an unresolved vulnerability
- Files, logs, paths, or screen information that should not be disclosed to third parties

If authentication information is posted by mistake, promptly revoke or change it in addition to removing the post.

## 3. Private Inquiries

The official GeneSIS contact accepts inquiries concerning purchases, Founder verification, terms, rights, privacy, non-public defects, and other matters unsuitable for public posting.

Suggested subject prefixes:

```text
[Founder] Founder status or purchase verification
[Rights] Terms or rights inquiry
[Privacy] Personal information inquiry
[Private Bug] Non-public defect report
[Other] Other private inquiry
```

For Founder verification, GeneSIS may request information such as a BOOTH order number, product name, or approximate purchase date.

GeneSIS will not request passwords, two-factor authentication codes, backup codes, credit card numbers, or other authentication or payment credentials.

For personal information handling and privacy requests, see the [Privacy Notice](./PRIVACY_NOTICE.md).

## 4. Security Reports

Do not post unresolved security issues in public Issues or on social media.

When GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting is enabled, use that route. If it is unavailable, contact the official GeneSIS address with the subject `[Security Report]`.

The [Security Policy](../SECURITY.md) governs scope, report contents, and disclosure handling.

## 5. Support Scope

Depending on the circumstances, GeneSIS may:

- Review general defects and reproduction conditions
- Provide information about Known Limitations or workarounds
- Correct or supplement documentation
- Consider corrected or updated releases
- Confirm official distribution files and verification information
- Provide guidance on terms, rights, or Founder status

Unless separately stated, support does not include:

- Installation or remote configuration for an individual computer
- Support for third-party products such as Ollama, models, operating systems, or hardware
- Individual creation of Personas, prompts, Knowledge, or References
- Conversion, organization, or recovery of conversation history or other data
- Customization for an individual environment
- Ongoing individual consulting
- Provision of source code, internal materials, or unreleased builds

G.E.N.E is used together with external runtimes, models, operating systems, and hardware. GeneSIS does not control or guarantee the specifications, failures, discontinuation, or response quality of those external products.

## 6. Response Policy

Response decisions primarily consider reproducibility, impact, severity, safety, risk of data loss, technical feasibility, and current development conditions.

The amount of support, Founder participation, BOOST, or number of purchases does not change technical assessment, correction priority, or the basic support scope.

The order of receipt may differ from the order of response.

GeneSIS is individually operated. Development, publication, replies, or support may be suspended, reduced, or discontinued because of health, living circumstances, funding, technical conditions, or changes to external platforms.

GeneSIS does not guarantee:

- A reply to every inquiry
- A reply within a specified period
- Individual handling
- Correction of an issue
- Adoption or implementation of a request
- A response deadline
- Recovery of user data
- A particular result

GeneSIS may limit or end communication in cases involving abuse, threats, harassment, spam, false information, requests for assistance with illegal conduct, or conduct that substantially disrupts operations. Legitimate criticism, defect reports, or differing opinions alone are not grounds for restriction.
