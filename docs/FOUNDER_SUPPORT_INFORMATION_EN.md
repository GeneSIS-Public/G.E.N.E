## 2. About Founder Support

Founder Support is a product through which purchasers can voluntarily participate in and support `Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1`, conducted by GeneSIS, with G.E.N.E serving as the entry point.

Founder is a limited participation category indicating an early supporter of Phase 1. Founder Support is not an equity investment, loan, profit-sharing investment, or joint business agreement.

Purchase does not grant ownership, copyright, Source Code Access rights, profit-sharing rights, voting rights, management participation rights, authority to determine development policy, or the right to require implementation of requests.

The BOOTH purchase record for Founder Support serves as the primary record for confirming Founder status.

BOOST is optional. Whether additional support is provided, and the amount of that support, does not change Founder status, provided deliverables, Support scope, technical evaluation of Feedback, correction priority, or influence over development.

For details, review the [Founder Terms](./FOUNDER_TERMS.md).

## 6. Delivery and Version Management

The Founder Initial Package can be obtained after the BOOTH order is completed and the product becomes available for Download.

Planned future deliverables and Additional Deliverables are added to the purchaser-only Download area for this Founder Support product after they have been established as deliverables for Founder distribution.

Provided Files are managed so that the contents of an existing File name are not silently changed. Version, Build, or Revision identifiers are used where appropriate.

Permanent storage by BOOTH or any other external Service is not guaranteed. Save downloaded Files in your own environment and create Backups as needed.

Eligible Updates, revisions, future Versions, and other distribution scope during Phase 1 are governed by the Founder Terms.

## 7. Support, Privacy, and Security

For publicly reportable general defects, Documentation Feedback, and Development Feedback, use the GitHub Issue Forms for the relevant Product.

Do not post purchase records, Founder status information, personal information, authentication information, private matters, or unresolved Security issues to a Public Issue.

Founder confirmation, deliverable distribution, and inquiry handling may use BOOTH order information or the minimum necessary information voluntarily provided by the purchaser.

Do not send Passwords, two-factor authentication Codes, Backup Codes, private keys, or complete payment information to GeneSIS.

For private inquiries and for information about the applicable inquiry scope, review the guidance in [Contact and Support](./CONTACT_AND_SUPPORT_EN.md) and use the appropriate official contact route.
