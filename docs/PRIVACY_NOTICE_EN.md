This Notice defines the basic policy for information handled by the GeneSIS-Operator in connection with GeneSIS, G.E.N.E v1.x, and Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1 Founder Support. If another Product has its own Privacy Notice, review that Notice as well for Product-specific use and inquiries.

## 1. Responsible Operator

GeneSIS is an individual project developed and operated by one GeneSIS-Operator.

The GeneSIS-Operator makes final decisions concerning information handling, inquiries, Founder verification, rights requests, and external responses.

**Official GeneSIS Contact**

`genesis.official.poc@mail.com`

Additional information required by applicable law will be provided through methods consistent with applicable law and the procedures of the relevant platform.

## 2. G.E.N.E and Local Data

G.E.N.E is a local AI dialogue environment that operates on the user's device.

During ordinary use, GeneSIS does not automatically collect or receive:

- Conversation history stored in G.E.N.E
- Personas
- Summaries
- Knowledge
- References
- Prompts
- Input to or output from a connected model
- Local files or storage paths
- API keys or other authentication information

When a user voluntarily sends such information for a defect report, inquiry, or support request, GeneSIS handles only the information actually submitted.

Before sending information, users should remove or redact unnecessary personal information, credentials, private conversations, and third-party information.

## 3. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on user actions and the platforms used, the GeneSIS-Operator may receive or view the following information.

### 3.1 Inquiries and Support

- Name, display name, or handle
- Email address
- Inquiry content
- Attached files
- Technical information such as version, build, OS, runtime, and model
- Logs, screen information, and reproduction steps
- Reply and handling history

### 3.2 BOOTH and Founder Verification

- Purchaser information displayed through BOOTH
- Order number
- Product name
- Purchase period
- Platform records relating to purchase, download, or messages
- Information reasonably necessary to verify Founder status

The GeneSIS-Operator does not directly process BOOTH payments and does not receive complete payment credentials such as full credit card numbers.

### 3.3 GitHub

- Public GitHub profile information
- Issues, comments, reports, and attachments
- Non-public information submitted through Private Vulnerability Reporting or similar routes
- Public activity records in the Repository

GitHub Issues are public. Do not post personal information, credentials, private conversations, or details of unresolved vulnerabilities.

### 3.4 note, X, and Other Official Media

- Public profile information
- Public information such as follows, reactions, replies, mentions, and comments
- Content voluntarily made public or sent by the user

Direct messages on X or other social media are not formal routes for support, Founder verification, privacy requests, or security reports.

### 3.5 Technical Records

A platform or email service may process access times, IP addresses, device information, cookies, and other technical records. Such information is handled under the privacy policy and settings of the relevant platform.

The GeneSIS-Operator does not necessarily obtain or control all information held by each platform.

## 4. Purposes of Use

Information is used only as reasonably necessary for the following purposes:

- Reviewing and responding to inquiries
- Investigating defects, compatibility, and security issues
- Improving documents, releases, Known Limitations, and support guidance
- Confirming official distribution files, purchase records, and Founder status
- Handling the EULA, Founder Terms, and other conditions
- Responding to privacy and rights requests
- Addressing abuse, spam, threats, harassment, or security risks
- Complying with applicable law
- Maintaining records reasonably necessary for GeneSIS development and operation

Changes to a purpose of use will be made only within a range reasonably related to the previous purpose, and this Notice will be updated when necessary.

## 5. AI Assistance

The GeneSIS-Operator may use AI to assist with document organization, translation, technical review, or organization of inquiry content.

When non-public information is handled with AI, the GeneSIS-Operator reduces the information to what is necessary and applies anonymization or redaction where appropriate. Passwords, authentication codes, private keys, and complete payment information are not entered into AI systems.

AI alone does not make final decisions or send final external responses concerning Founder status, rights, privacy requests, security handling, or other important matters. The GeneSIS-Operator reviews the final decision and outgoing content.

## 6. Third-Party Platforms and Service Providers

GeneSIS uses third-party services such as:

- BOOTH
- GitHub
- note
- X
- Email services
- AI services when used

When a user uses one of these platforms, the platform operator also processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Processing may occur outside Japan.

The GeneSIS-Operator does not sell personal data and does not improperly disclose it to third parties, except with the individual's consent, when required by law, when necessary to protect rights or safety, or when appropriately entrusting processing to a service provider necessary for operations.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary to fulfill the purposes of use, maintain handling records, respond to disputes, abuse, or security issues, and comply with applicable law.

Retention periods vary depending on the type of information, the matter being handled, platform specifications, and legal requirements.

When the purpose has ended and retention is no longer necessary, information is deleted or made less identifiable where reasonably possible. Information retained by BOOTH, GitHub, note, X, or another platform remains subject to that platform's functions and procedures.

## 8. Security Measures

Considering the nature of the information and the scale of individual operation, the GeneSIS-Operator uses measures such as:

- Appropriate, separate passwords for accounts
- Two-factor authentication where available
- Limiting accounts, devices, and connected applications with access
- Avoiding unnecessary collection of credentials or sensitive information
- Separating public and non-public information
- Organizing and deleting information that is no longer needed
- Taking appropriate action when a security issue is identified

Absolute security cannot be guaranteed when using the Internet and third-party platforms.

## 9. Access, Correction, and Other Requests

To the extent provided by applicable law, an individual may request notice of the purpose of use, access, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, erasure, or suspension of third-party provision concerning information about that individual.

A request should identify the information concerned and the requested action. The GeneSIS-Operator may request the minimum information necessary to confirm identity, protect third-party rights, maintain security, and prevent fraudulent requests.

A request may be denied in whole or in part when required or permitted by law, when it would harm the rights or safety of another person, when the relevant information is not held, or when the GeneSIS-Operator lacks authority to delete or modify information held by a platform.

## 10. Data Incident Response

When a leak, loss, damage, or other incident involving personal information is identified, the GeneSIS-Operator reviews the scope and may consider access restriction, credential changes, notice to affected individuals, reports to the platform, reports to relevant authorities, or other appropriate action.

Do not post unresolved security issues in public Issues or on social media. Use the private route in the [Security Policy](../SECURITY_EN.md).

## 11. Changes to This Notice

This Notice may be updated in response to changes in law, platforms, operational methods, information handling, or deliverables.

When a material change is made, GeneSIS will provide notice where reasonably possible through GitHub, BOOTH, note, X, or another appropriate official medium.

The updated content is governed by the version, status, and effective date displayed in this Notice.
