## Article 1 — Purpose and Nature of Founder Support

These Terms define the participation conditions, deliverables, relationship between Founders and GeneSIS, and other necessary matters concerning “Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1 Founder Support” offered by GeneSIS on BOOTH.

Founder Support is not an upgraded edition of G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0, an edition with added functions, a priority support contract, an equity investment, a loan, or a profit-sharing product.

Founder Support is a product through which a purchaser voluntarily and expressly participates in and supports the Phase 1 activities of Project: Code-NOAH and GeneSIS, with G.E.N.E serving as the point of entry.

## Article 2 — Definitions

1. **G.E.N.E** means the local AI dialogue environment developed by GeneSIS and its related deliverables.

2. **Phase 1** means the G.E.N.E v1.x development and public operational stage in which G.E.N.E uses an LLM through an API-server tool such as Ollama.

3. **Founder** means a purchaser who reviews these Terms and expressly indicates an intention to participate in and support Phase 1 by purchasing the Founder Support product on BOOTH.

4. **Founder Deliverables** means O-type Generator Founder Edition and Gene-ScrIbe ConSole Founder Edition, which these Terms define as later deliverables for Phase 1 Founders.

5. **Additional Support** means optional financial support through BOOST during the BOOTH purchase procedure or another route formally announced by GeneSIS.

## Article 3 — Agreement and Founder Status

1. GeneSIS will provide the full text of these Terms, or a clear route to review the full text before purchase, on the Founder Support product page.

2. Before purchase, a prospective purchaser must review these Terms, the product description, the G.E.N.E EULA, supported environments, Known Limitations, and information concerning delivery timing for Founder Deliverables.

   The G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0 EULA is available [here](./EULA.md) as a pre-purchase review copy. The linked EULA is the Japanese authoritative text. Use of the G.E.N.E application is governed by `EULA.txt` in the official distribution archive.

3. Completion of the Founder Support purchase procedure constitutes agreement to these Terms and participation as a Phase 1 Founder.

4. A person who obtains G.E.N.E for free may later become a Founder by purchasing Founder Support while new Phase 1 Founder participation remains open.

5. Support provided only through BOOST or another route does not establish Founder status, regardless of amount, unless the Founder Support product is purchased.

6. A minor must obtain consent from a legal representative before purchase.

Founder status is based on the express act of selecting and purchasing the program, not on the amount of support.

## Article 4 — Price, BOOST, and the Free Release

1. The Founder Support product price is **JPY 2,000**.

2. When BOOST is available during BOOTH checkout, a purchaser may provide optional additional support.

3. Additional support does not change Founder status, Founder Deliverables, support scope, technical evaluation of feedback, correction priority, or influence over development.

4. The JPY 2,000 amount is the price of Founder Support, not the price of G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0 itself.

5. G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0 is provided free of charge, without functional restriction, as the same official application archive with the same build and SHA-256 for Founders and non-Founders.

6. The corresponding G.E.N.E application does not differ in functionality between Founders and free users.

7. Founder Support is a participation and support product for sustaining the Phase 1 activities of the individually developed GeneSIS project. Support is treated as sustaining the activity base as a whole, including development, verification, operation, distribution, and documentation. GeneSIS does not promise full allocation to a specific purpose, separate accounting by purpose, or individual financial reporting.

## Article 5 — Items Available at Purchase and Later Deliverables

1. Founder Support includes an initial Founder package available at purchase and the later deliverables defined in this Article.

2. The initial Founder package will, as a general rule, include:

   - The official G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0 application archive with the same build and SHA-256 as the free distribution
   - README FIRST
   - These Founder Support Terms
   - Founder Delivery Policy
   - Package Manifest
   - Detached signature for the Package Manifest
   - Release-signing public-key information
   - SHA-256 list

3. GeneSIS defines the following two items as **later deliverables** for Phase 1 Founders and will provide them after establishing them as official Founder distributions:

   1. Persona authoring support tool  
      **O-type Generator Founder Edition**

   2. Log formatting and migration support tool  
      **Gene-ScrIbe ConSole Founder Edition**

4. No specific completion date, delivery date, delivery month, or development period is guaranteed for the two deliverables.

5. At present, the two deliverables are positioned as limited, non-sale deliverables for Phase 1 Founders and are not principally intended for general free distribution or separate ordinary sale.

6. This Article does not guarantee that the two deliverables will never be made available to non-Founders in the future. Future distribution methods may be reconsidered in response to changes in technology, standards, external services, rights, or development policy.

## Article 6 — Scope of Founder Deliverables

Phase 1 Founders are entitled to:

1. The first official distribution of O-type Generator Founder Edition
2. The first official distribution of Gene-ScrIbe ConSole Founder Edition
3. Updates and corrections to the preceding two tools that GeneSIS establishes and publishes as official Founder distributions during Phase 1

The following are excluded:

- Internal operational versions used by GeneSIS
- Development builds or incomplete test versions
- Non-public branches
- Source code or internal design materials
- Customization for an individual user
- Support for every external AI service or input format
- Guaranteed reproduction of every Persona
- Versions established after transition to Phase 2
- Phase 2 or Phase 3 deliverables
- Successor products redesigned as separate projects
- Perpetual updates or indefinite support

The GeneSIS-Operator determines whether an item has become an official Founder distribution after reviewing quality, distributability, safety, rights, documentation, and release status.

## Article 7 — Development Priority and Timing

1. After the public release of G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0, preparation of Founder Deliverables will be treated as a major subsequent Phase 1 development activity.

2. Work involving security, critical defects, legal compliance, distribution continuity, or other urgent matters may take priority.

3. Although the Founder Deliverables are based on existing internal operational work, they require removal of internal-specific information, adjustment of UI and wording, organization of terms, testing, packaging, rights review, and release procedures before general use.

4. GeneSIS is developed and operated primarily by one GeneSIS-Operator. Development timing may vary because of funding, living circumstances, health, technical issues, verification results, or external platform conditions.

5. No specific completion date, delivery date, delivery month, or development period is guaranteed.

6. GeneSIS will not distribute a deliverable with a known material quality, safety, or rights issue merely to meet a deadline.

7. At the same time, the policy is to release a deliverable without unnecessary delay once the defined minimum distribution criteria have been satisfied.

## Article 8 — Delivery and Acquisition Records

1. The initial Founder package, Founder Deliverables, and updates will generally be added as version-identifiable files to the BOOTH download area for Founder Support purchasers.

2. A Founder is responsible for maintaining the BOOTH account, purchase history, and environment required to obtain deliverables.

3. If new participation ends or the product becomes non-public, GeneSIS will, to the extent permitted by BOOTH functionality, maintain access for existing Founders to deliverables already provided through purchase history.

4. Permanent storage by BOOTH or another external service is not guaranteed. Founders should save obtained deliverables in their own environment and create backups where appropriate.

5. Except where required for rights, legal, or security reasons, GeneSIS will endeavor not to remove already provided deliverables without notice.

6. Availability and material updates may be announced through official media such as BOOTH, GitHub, note, or X.

7. When ordinary delivery through BOOTH is impracticable because of special circumstances, GeneSIS may verify Founder status and purchase records individually through the official GeneSIS contact.

1. The authoritative record for Founder status is, as a general rule, the BOOTH purchase record for Founder Support.

2. The GeneSIS-Operator may use order information or user identifiers available through BOOTH only as necessary for Founder administration, delivery, inquiries, or verification for acknowledgements.

3. When verification is required, GeneSIS may request an order number, product name, approximate purchase date, or other information reasonably capable of confirming purchase.

4. Send verification information only to the official GeneSIS contact described in [Contact and Support](./CONTACT_AND_SUPPORT.md), and only to the minimum extent necessary.

5. Do not send passwords, two-factor authentication codes, backup codes, private keys, or complete payment information.

1. Founder status is treated as one set of rights per purchaser.

4. Founder status and the right to receive undelivered Founder Deliverables may not, as a general rule, be transferred, resold, loaned, or shared with a third party.

5. Founder Deliverables may not be distributed, shared, resold, or published in violation of the terms applicable to each deliverable.

1. New Phase 1 Founder participation will be accepted during the public operation of G.E.N.E Phase 1 / v1.x.

5. When material delay, long-term suspension, or a policy change affects Founder Deliverables, GeneSIS will provide information on the current status and outlook to the extent reasonably possible.

6. If continuation becomes difficult because of health, living circumstances, funding, disaster, external service suspension, technical failure, or another circumstance, GeneSIS will provide information on undelivered items, provided content, and future handling to the extent reasonably possible.

7. Nothing in this paragraph limits any purchaser right available under applicable law.

8. A separate Founder program, support program, sales method, or participation terms may be designed for Phase 2 or later. Phase 1 Founders do not participate automatically.
