## Article 1 — Purpose and Nature of Founder Support

These Terms define the participation conditions, provided content, relationship between Founders and GeneSIS, and other necessary matters concerning “Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1 Founder Support” offered by GeneSIS on BOOTH.

Founder Support is not an upgraded edition of G.E.N.E Alpha v1.0, an edition with added functions, a priority Support contract, an equity investment, a loan, or a profit-sharing product.

Founder Support is a product through which a purchaser voluntarily and expressly participates in and supports the activities of Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1 conducted by GeneSIS, with G.E.N.E serving as the entry point.

## Article 2 — Definitions and the Relationship of Phases

For these Terms, the following definitions apply.

1. **Project: Code-NOAH**
   The Project conducted by GeneSIS and the development and public-operation framework that includes G.E.N.E and Product, Tool, Operation, and other deliverables established under the Project.

2. **G.E.N.E**
   The local AI dialogue environment developed by GeneSIS as a direct deliverable of Project: Code-NOAH, together with its related deliverables.

3. **Project Phase**
   A stage representing the development and public operation of Project: Code-NOAH as a whole. Authority for the Phase resides with Project: Code-NOAH.

4. **G.E.N.E Phase**
   A G.E.N.E-side expression synchronized with the Project Phase. A G.E.N.E Phase is not a separate Phase system and represents the same stage as the Project Phase with the same number.

5. **Phase 1**
   Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1. On the G.E.N.E side it is synchronously expressed as G.E.N.E Phase 1 and currently includes the development and public-operation stage of the G.E.N.E v1.x line.

6. **Phase Deliverable**
   A Product, Tool, Operation, or other deliverable established under Project: Code-NOAH during the applicable Project Phase. Merely being a Phase Deliverable does not make an item part of Founder Support.

7. **Founder**
   A purchaser who reviews these Terms and purchases the Founder Support product on BOOTH, thereby expressly indicating an intention to participate in and support Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1.

8. **Founder Deliverables**
   O-type Generator Founder Edition and Gene-ScrIbe ConSole Founder Edition, which these Terms expressly define as later deliverables for Phase 1 Founders.

9. **Supplemental / Additional Deliverable**
   A deliverable separate from the Founder Deliverables that GeneSIS expressly identifies through BOOTH, Founder Support Information, or another official medium as an additional item for Founder Support purchasers. An item does not become a Supplemental / Additional Deliverable merely because it was established during Phase 1.

10. **Additional Support**
    Optional financial support through BOOST during BOOTH checkout or another route formally announced by GeneSIS.

Unless separately stated, Project: Code-NOAH deliverables other than G.E.N.E are not treated as having their own Project Phase and are managed by their respective Version, Build, or Revision.

## Article 6 — Scope of Founder Deliverables and Additional Deliverables

The Founder Deliverables provided to Phase 1 Founders under these Terms are:

1. The first official distribution release of O-type Generator Founder Edition
2. The first official distribution release of Gene-ScrIbe ConSole Founder Edition
3. Updates and corrections to those two items that GeneSIS establishes and publishes as official Founder distribution releases during Phase 1

The scope of a Supplemental / Additional Deliverable follows the details expressly announced by GeneSIS for that item. Unless separately stated, it does not include all future Major Versions, successor products, perpetual Updates, or indefinite Support for that item.

Unless expressly stated in these Terms or an individual Additional Deliverable announcement, Founder Support does not include:

- Every Phase Deliverable established during Project: Code-NOAH Phase 1
- Internal GeneSIS operational editions
- Development builds or incomplete test editions
- Unpublished Branches
- Source Code or internal design materials
- Individual user-specific Customization
- Support for every external AI Service or input format
- Guaranteed reproduction of every Persona
- Versions or deliverables established after transition to Phase 2
- Successor products redesigned as a separate Project
- Perpetual Updates or indefinite Support

The GeneSIS-Operator determines whether an item has been established as an official Founder distribution release or Additional Deliverable after considering quality, distributability, safety, rights, explanatory materials, and Release status.

## Article 7 — Development Priority and Delivery Timing

1. Development, verification, operation, distribution, and Documentation work during Phase 1 proceeds in consideration of Project: Code-NOAH as a whole. Coordination of Founder Deliverables is treated as one of the principal later development areas within Phase 1.

2. Security, material defects, legal compliance, maintenance of distribution environments, and other urgent work may take priority.

3. Founder Deliverables may be based on internal operational deliverables, but public distribution may require removal of internal information, UI and wording changes, organization of terms, testing, packaging, rights review, and Release work.

4. GeneSIS is developed and operated primarily by one GeneSIS-Operator. Development periods may vary because of funding, living circumstances, health, technical problems, verification results, or external Platform conditions.

5. No specific completion date, delivery date, delivery month, or development period is guaranteed for Founder Deliverables.

6. GeneSIS will not distribute a deliverable known to have a material quality, safety, or rights problem merely to meet a deadline.

7. At the same time, once a deliverable satisfies the defined minimum distribution standard, the policy is to Release it without unnecessary delay.

8. Supplemental / Additional Deliverables do not automatically replace the development priority or delivery obligations of the Founder Deliverables. An individual additional delivery does not change the meaning or definition of the Founder Deliverables stated in Article 5.3.

3. Founders are responsible for maintaining their BOOTH Account, purchase history, and environment necessary to obtain deliverables.

4. If new participation closes or the product becomes private, GeneSIS intends, to the extent permitted by BOOTH specifications, to maintain access for existing Founders to already provided deliverables through their purchase history.

7. Availability or material updates of Founder Deliverables and Supplemental / Additional Deliverables may be announced through BOOTH, GitHub, note, X, or another official medium.

8. If ordinary delivery through BOOTH becomes difficult under special circumstances, GeneSIS may individually confirm Founder status and purchase records through the official GeneSIS contact route.

Participation in Founder Support does not grant:

- Ownership of GeneSIS or Project: Code-NOAH

Founder Support is not a financial instrument, equity agreement, profit-sharing investment, or joint venture agreement.
